    About the program

    The accelerated B.S. Informatics program at Indiana University is perfect for students who want to understand how technology affects society. This degree teaches you to create innovative solutions for today and tomorrow through hands-on projects that connect theory with real-world applications, focusing on data analysis, user experience design, and software development. You’ll collaborate with classmates from different backgrounds to solve complex problems. The program also enhances your critical thinking and problem-solving skills, preparing you for the fast-paced tech world. Plus, you’ll network with industry professionals through internships and events, giving you an advantage in the job market. By graduation, you’ll be ready for various tech careers, from software engineering to data science, while making a positive impact on society.

    Key takeaways

    • Core focus

      The curriculum connects people, information, and technology to solve problems in areas like healthcare, privacy, and security.

    • Practical skills

    • Specializations & concentrations

      Choose a concentration that matches your interests and career goals such as:

      • Business

        Learn the foundational principles of economics, management, and marketing to lead organizations and drive global commerce.

      • Health Information Management

        Master the systems and technologies necessary to manage and secure patient health data, bridging healthcare and information technology.

      • Legal Informatics

        Explore the intersection of law and technology, using computational methods to solve legal problems, manage information, and streamline judicial processes.

      • Sustainability

        Gain the knowledge to develop and implement practices that balance environmental, social, and economic needs for a resilient future.

      • Enterprise Resource Planning

        Focus on integrating and optimizing a company’s core business processes—like finance, HR, and supply chain—using comprehensive software systems.

    • Career pathways

      The degree prepares graduates for diverse career opportunities, including:

      • User Experience Designer

        A user experience designer creates intuitive and engaging digital experiences by researching user needs and designing user-friendly interfaces.

        $85k – $130k

      • Digital Library Specialist

        A digital library specialist curates and manages digital resources, ensuring easy access to information while supporting users in navigating technology and data.

        $90k – $160k

      • Network Manager

        A network manager oversees the design, implementation, and maintenance of an organization’s computer networks, ensuring optimal performance and security.

        $75k – $140k

      • Web Developer

        A web developer creates and maintains websites, ensuring they are user-friendly, visually appealing, and function smoothly across various devices.

        $85k – $130k

      • Information Architect

        An information architect designs and organizes information systems to enhance user experience and ensure efficient data flow within an organization.

        $90k – $160k

    • Experiential learning

      Those accepted into IU Online benefit from comprehensive personalized support services.
